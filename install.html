Install it on your device

The steps depend on your device. We highlighted the one that matches yours.

Your device

Android

Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet
  1. Tap Install the app on this page.
  2. Confirm with Install.
  3. Open STEM Collaboration from your home screen.

No button? Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Your device

iPhone and iPad

Safari only
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Your device

Computer

Chrome or Edge
  1. Click Install the app on this page.
  2. Or click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. The app opens in its own window.

About the app

  • CostFree for every student.
  • SizeVery small. It uses almost no storage on your device.
  • UpdatesAutomatic. You always have the latest version.
  • Sign inWith your student ID and password, the same account as the website.
  • InternetNeeded for chat, teams and announcements. The app shell opens even when you are offline.
  • Remove itLike any app: press and hold the icon, or uninstall it from your browser.
