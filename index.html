A shared workspace for Egypt's STEM students.

STEM Collaboration brings students from every STEM school in Egypt onto one platform, so projects, questions and ideas can move between campuses.

Sign inCreate an account

Why this platform exists

STEM schools teach through research, inquiry and capstone projects. The work is ambitious, but each campus mostly works on its own.

STEM Collaboration connects those campuses. A team in Alexandria can learn from a project built in Assiut, and a student in Sharqia can ask a question that someone in Giza has already solved.

What it is built for

Your school community

A live chat for the students of your own school, with photos, links and emoji reactions.

Announcements

Important news from the platform team, shown on your dashboard as soon as you sign in.

Competitions

Upcoming STEM competitions with dates, photos and full details, sorted by field such as robotics, programming and math.

Finding teammates

Meet students across schools who share your interests, for competitions, research and capstone work.

Getting started

  1. Get your student IDYour school issues it. It is the only ID the platform uses.
  2. Create your accountEnter your full name, choose your school, add your student ID and phone number, and set a password.
  3. Sign inFrom then on, sign in with your student ID and password.
